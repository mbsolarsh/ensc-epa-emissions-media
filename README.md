# Two Lanes, One Story

**ENSC 1000: Environmental Issues in the Media, Option #2 Visual Explainer**

An interactive website comparing how **NPR** (left-leaning) and **Fox News** (right-leaning) covered the EPA's February 2026 repeal of the greenhouse gas *endangerment finding* and federal vehicle emissions standards.

**Topic:** EPA vehicle emissions standards (Air Pollution & Climate Change)

## Articles
| Lean | Outlet | Headline | Author(s) | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Left | NPR | [Trump's EPA will stop regulating greenhouse gases, setting up a legal fight](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/11/nx-s1-5678273/trump-epa-climate-change-endangerment) | Jeff Brady & Camila Domonoske | Feb 11–12, 2026 |
| Right | Fox News | [Obama-era greenhouse gas rules gone as EPA's Zeldin signs 'single largest deregulatory action' in history](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/obama-era-greenhouse-gas-rules-gone-epas-zeldin-signs-single-largest-deregulatory-action-history) | Charles Creitz | Feb 12, 2026 |

## What's on the site
- Timeline of the endangerment finding
- Article cards with bias ratings and screenshot "receipts"
- **Headline Lens**: toggle between how each outlet opens the story
- **Framing breakdown**: the five Step 3 questions (headline, facts vs. opinion, tone, selection/omission, framing)
- Emphasis meter and loaded-word explorer
- **What's Missing?** flip cards
- **Who Said It?** quiz
- Science facts from EPA, NOAA, and the American Lung Association, plus a tailpipe CO₂ calculator
- Individual and collective action checklist
- Artist's note (300+ words)

## Files
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`: the website (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step)
- `images/`: add `npr-screenshot.png` and `fox-screenshot.png` (headline screenshots with date/timestamp)

To view locally, double-click `index.html`.
